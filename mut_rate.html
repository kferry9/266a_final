Big question: Can we use dimethyl sulfate (DMS) structure probing techniques to accurately determine pair bases in in vitro transcribed human telomerasa RNA (hTR)?

Data: Collected from hTR modified through DMS probing. DMS modification induces base mismatch modification during reverse-transcription which is mapped by Illumina short read sequencing.
